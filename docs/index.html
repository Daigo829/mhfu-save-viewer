Items

Every item slot as stored in the save, in on-screen order — the 1000-slot item box (10 pages of 100) and the 24-slot pouch (3 pages of 8), plus a tracker for holding every obtainable item at 99. Slots are not packed, so occupied slots can sit after empty ones; both lists walk every slot. More than 99 of an item means more than one slot, so counts are shown per slot rather than totalled.

▸ What is this tracker for?

This tracker is designed for players that want to get every x99-able item to 99, which means 1020 of the game's 1025 storable items.

▸ What is a Grindable Item?

A Grindable item is one you must personally work to acquire. It excludes items that can be mass purchased with Zenny or Pokke Points and anything you can craft from purchasable parts, instead they have to come from quest rewards, gathering, carving, part breaks, monster drops, Pokke Farm, Trenya, Veggie Elder, cookable or craftable, but from farmed ingredients.

Quest counts — internal byte order

The quest counters are stored at consecutive offsets starting at 0x672D8, in save order (which differs from the on-screen order). This maps each array slot to its raw offset, byte width, the category it holds, and its live value (quests cleared in that category).

Weapon usage — internal byte order

The weapon-usage array at 0x678E0 (11 × u16) is stored in an internal weapon order that differs from the on-screen bar order. This maps each array slot to its raw offset, the weapon it holds, and its live value (quests cleared with that weapon).

Equipment

Every slot of the 1000-slot equipment box, in slot order. Each record stores its category and item id; name, class, rarity, attack and the G-weapon / rarity-9–10 flags come from the equipment table. Attack is a weapon value — the table carries no defence figures for armour, so those rows show a dash. Per-piece detail — decorations, sharpness, skills, upgrade path — is coming in a later pass.
